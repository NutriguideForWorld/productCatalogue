### Company Details

Effective **wellness**, **skincare**, and **aromatherapy** formulations begin with choosing the right botanical extracts. 
Pure, potent ingredients can elevate the aroma, efficacy, and therapeutic value of just about any product. 
Two such ingredients that have long been prized for their **natural healing** and aromatic properties are **essential oils** and herbal oils. 

**DigitalAssets**, based in India, is a leading supplier and exporter of **100% Pure Essential Oils**, **Therapeutic Herbal Oils**, **Cold-Pressed** Carrier Oils, and **Bulk Aromatic Extracts** sourced from ethically cultivated, **farm-fresh** botanicals nurtured under ideal climatic conditions. 
**Just a few drops of our pure essential or herbal oils are all that is needed to craft deeply aromatic and effective formulations** enjoyed by consumers worldwide. 
These high-grade extracts are also widely demanded by businesses **manufacturing cosmetics**, **personal care** items, **pharmaceuticals**, and **wellness products**.  

Supporting local Indian farmers and harnessing the rich botanical diversity of Indian soil, DigitalAssets delivers unmatched **purity**, **potency**, and natural goodness to the global market. 

Not only do we follow **sustainable cultivation** and **precision extraction** methods, but we also enforce strict product testing and certification standards that make our range one of the finest in the international market.

Production at DigitalAssets begins with certified raw botanicals processed in **state-of-the-art distillation and extraction** facilities. 

While we fulfill large-scale bulk orders every month, we never let volume override product quality. 

Throughout the manufacturing process, random samples undergo rigorous analysis—including **GC-MS** (Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry) and **strict pesticide testing**—to ensure our **quality and purity** standards meet international norms.

### Facilities
Only organically cultivated, mature, and nutrient-rich herbs, flowers, roots, and seeds go into our range of essential and herbal oils, bringing the authentic essence of Indian botanicals directly to every customer. 

DigitalAssets follows a sustainable development strategy for manufacturing and exporting large batches of bulk drums and retail-ready bottles. Specialists at our purpose-built plant ensure uniform aroma, chemical profile, and consistency from batch to batch. 
Plant debris, sediments, and impurities are carefully filtered out, and every batch is verified for overall purity using advanced testing methodologies in a dedicated quality-control lab. Only oils that are potent, additive-free, safe for use, have optimal shelf life, and are securely **packed in leak-proof**, **UV-protected**, **airtight packaging** (ranging from 15ml, 50ml, and 100ml bottles to 1kg, 5kg, and 25kg aluminum/HDPE drums) are approved for release and export. 
QualityAs soon as aromatic herbs and flowers are harvested, their volatile compounds can degrade quickly, making immediate and careful processing critical to preserving their therapeutic profile. Botanicals are handpicked, sorted, and distilled in strict accordance with international quality requirements. Rigorous sanitary conditions are maintained at our facility without cutting corners, and every batch undergoes comprehensive testing. Among the **vital factors analyzed are aroma profile**, **refractive index**, **optical rotation**, **specific gravity**, and **active botanical constituents**.
